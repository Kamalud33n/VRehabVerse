How a session runs

From headset to clinical report, without a therapist touching a spreadsheet.

01 — Start

One short code links the headset to the patient

The therapist generates a session code before the child puts the headset on. It expires in 15 minutes, so it can never be reused later or accidentally linked to the wrong patient.

02 — Supervise

Watch reps, range of motion, and accuracy as they happen

The headset streams live metrics and camera POV frames over WebSocket as the child plays. The therapist follows the skeleton tracking and real numbers on their tablet — not a summary written after the session ends.

03 — Track

Every session adds to the patient's progress chart

ROM progression, accuracy, left-vs-right hand usage, and reaction time trend across every session automatically — so a therapist can see whether this week's plan is actually working before the next visit.

04 — Report

A signed clinical PDF, ready before the parents leave the room

Session, weekly, monthly, and comparative reports generate automatically with KPI cards and progress charts. The supervising specialist's name is pulled straight from the login — no retyping.
